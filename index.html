About KV Furniture

Furniture with character.

Since May 2022, KV Furniture has grown through one simple belief — if our name is on it, we should be proud of how it was made.

We manufacture furniture to order in our own workshop, combining premium materials, thoughtful craftsmanship and custom designs.

From statement sofas and royal dining furniture to bedroom collections and completely customised pieces, every product is created to become a meaningful part of someone's home.

Our approach is simple: honest materials, careful craftsmanship and transparent pricing without unnecessary compromises.
